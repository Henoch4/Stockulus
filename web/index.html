How it stays neutral: four steps between an idea and a real trade, in order, every time.

1

Read the market

Stockulus compares a tokenized stock's price to its real-world spot price, factoring in dividend yield and borrow cost. When the gap is wide enough and steady enough, that's a signal.

stock_carry.py
2

Check the limits

A separate safety gate — not the trading logic itself — checks position size, daily loss budget, and confidence against hard limits before anything is allowed through.

risk_gate.py
3

Write it down first

The decision — asset, size, confidence, the reasoning — is logged on Solana before the trade executes. Anyone can check the record; no one, including Stockulus' own operator, can rewrite it after the fact.

trade_audit_trail
4

Trade it

Only then does the real swap happen, and the fill gets checked against what was logged. A mismatch beyond tolerance halts the agent rather than quietly absorbing the difference.

meteora_executor.py

Carry, not conviction.

Tokenized stocks trade around the clock on Solana, but real markets close. That mismatch — plus dividends and the cost of holding — creates a small, persistent gap between a tokenized stock's price and its real one. Stockulus doesn't bet on where a stock is going. It holds the gap, collects the carry, and gets out when the gap closes.

The harder problem isn't finding that gap — it's proving, to someone who's never met you, that the system trading it actually behaves the way it says it does. That's what the audit trail and the risk gate are for.
