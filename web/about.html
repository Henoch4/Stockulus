About

Stockulus means the gap. That's the whole idea.

In finance, delta is how much a position moves when the thing it's tracking moves. Delta-neutral means holding that movement flat — profiting from the structure of a market instead of guessing its direction. That's the entire strategy, and it's why the name stuck.

Carry is boring on purpose.

Most trading agents are built to predict something — a price, a trend, a headline. Stockulus doesn't. It looks for a specific, structural gap: a tokenized stock trading away from its real-world price because tokenized markets never close and real ones do. That gap is small, it's persistent, and it doesn't require being right about the future — only patient about the present.

Boring is the point. A strategy that doesn't need conviction is a strategy that doesn't need to lie to you about how confident it is.

Principles: what actually shapes every decision in this system.

Log first, trade second

The decision goes on-chain before the trade happens, not after. That ordering is the whole trust model — a system that logs after the fact is just narrating its own choices.

Limits that can tighten, never loosen

An operator can make the safety limits stricter. Nothing — not the operator, not the agent — can make them looser than the last approved value. Ratcheting one direction only is what makes a limit mean something over time.

The trader can't also be the referee

Signal logic, risk limits, and the audit log are three separate pieces of code with no authority over each other. A bug or a bad incentive in one can't quietly override the other two.

Say what's actually true

If something isn't live yet, the docs and the dashboard say so, plainly — not "coming soon," an honest zero. A demo that has to lie to look impressive isn't a demo worth trusting.

How the last mile gets funded.

Stockulus' own token (STCKLS) and its stock-paired liquidity pool are the last piece — they need a small amount of funding to launch (0.15 SOL). Contributors get a pro-rata share of the token at launch and a permanent spot on the supporters wall on the dashboard.

If the goal is reached but launch doesn't happen within 14 days, contributions are returned in full. Every fee the live system earns afterward is tracked toward buying that token back — visible on the dashboard, verifiable against a transaction hash, not just a claimed number.
